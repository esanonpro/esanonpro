## Research

I use research projects to explore the trade-offs behind ML and statistical systems: **accuracy, computation, supervision, robustness and reproducibility**.

### [Weakly-Supervised Image Denoising with Transformers](https://github.com/esanonpro/transformer-image-denoising)

Integration of a transformer-based denoiser into a weakly-supervised pipeline for Poisson–Gaussian noise. The work introduces **SCUNet2**, evaluates it with variance-stabilizing transforms and compares it with strong CNN baselines.

`Swin Transformer · SCUNet2 · Noise2VST · Computer Vision · Self-Supervised Learning`

### [Fast Calibration of GARCH Models](https://github.com/esanonpro/fast-garch-calibration)

Study of a **Le Cam one-step estimator** for faster GARCH calibration, with theoretical analysis, 2,000-replication Monte Carlo experiments and an application to S&P 500 data.

`GARCH · Statistical Inference · Monte Carlo · Time Series · Computational Statistics`
